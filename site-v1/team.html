Rick Bingham

Insurance Expert & GIC Principal

As a seasoned senior-level insurance, reinsurance, regulatory, and run-off practitioner with over 40 years of experience, Rick possesses a demonstrated ability to lead diverse teams of professionals to new levels of success. His credentials as a subject matter expert have been accepted in both state and federal legal jurisdictions, and he has served as a hearing officer with the Attorney Registration and Disciplinary Commission. His accomplishments span claims handling, management, staff training, regulatory compliance, expert report drafting, and testimony.

Insurance Regulatory Attorney & Case Mapping Specialist

Insurance Regulatory Attorney
