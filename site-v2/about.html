About GIC

Experience that
delivers results

Founded in 2008 by Rick Bingham for one purpose: sharing decades of experience to help clients achieve success.

On every assignment, the team at Glynloen Insurance Consulting uses decades of in-depth insurance industry experience to deliver outstanding value to our clients. We are experts in our fields, and it is our mission to deliver the results your project deserves.

With almost 40 years in the insurance industry, there isn't much with which Rick hasn't been involved. In GIC's more than a dozen years of operations, our engagements have included expert witness services, reserve studies, policy assessments, claims audits and adjudications, and underwriting reviews. Based upon lessons learned during almost 20 years working with numerous Departments of Insurance, Rick has been instrumental in addressing challenges faced by his clients across a wide range of regulatory areas.

During those four decades of working in every part of the industry, Rick has also assembled and managed diverse teams of subject matter experts to address the industry's emerging challenges — including insurtech innovation, data privacy and security issues, as well as traditional and non-traditional insurance product compliance issues.
