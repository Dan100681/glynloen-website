Expert Witness Services

The GIC Process

At Glynloen Insurance Consulting (GIC) we take pride in digging deeper and providing more comprehensive analysis than other experts in the industry. On every assignment, GIC strives to provide the Court with the results of a thorough investigation into the insurance-related issues at the center of the litigation.

Review the Litigation

First, GIC reviews the pleadings to identify the key allegations in the litigation, so as to focus the analysis on examining those matters.

Analyze the Record

Next, GIC conducts an exhaustive review of the record to render preliminary opinions and focus additional analysis and research.

Present the Findings

Finally, Rick and the GIC team synthesize the findings into a report with detailed citations and demonstratives, communicated effectively to the Court.
