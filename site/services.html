Regulatory Consulting

Mr. Bingham, assisted by the Glynloen Insurance Consulting team, fully enjoys all aspects of regulatory consulting. Based upon Rick's experience working for and on behalf of many Departments of Insurance, GIC possesses unique skills and capacities for dealing with market conduct challenges, mock examinations, and the development and propagation of new processes.

Representative Engagements

  • Retained by a Property and Casualty carrier to assist in defending a multi-state Market Conduct Examination in which the client was accused of violating rules and regulations in all 50 states. The engagement lasted over five years and required numerous appearances before the NAIC's Market Actions Working Group (MAWG), hearings at State Departments of Insurance, and on-site dealings with state Market Conduct Examiners. Duties included writing a Corrective Action Plan (CAP) and a Regulatory Settlement Agreement (RSA), and designing and directing a mock examination based on the tests prescribed in the NAIC's Market Regulation Handbook.
  • Engaged by the Illinois Department of Insurance to perform a targeted Market Conduct examination of an Illinois-domiciled reinsurer — examining the ceding commission structure, operating expense budget, price and rating program (including questions of capacity and surplus requirements), and provisions of the company's reinsurance agreements. Performed pursuant to an Examination Warrant signed by the Director of Insurance and resulting in a Final Report of Examination.
  • Retained by a Property and Casualty carrier to assist in responding to a Department of Justice subpoena seeking data, records and responses to interrogatories.
  • Designed and conducted mock market conduct exams for multiple Accident & Health and Property & Casualty insurers.
